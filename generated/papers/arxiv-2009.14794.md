## 主張

出典のリンクは原論文PDFの該当ページを開く。リンクにカーソルを合わせると原文の引用が表示される。

- **c1** The paper introduces Performers, Transformer architectures that estimate regular softmax full-rank attention with provable accuracy using linear space and time complexity and no sparsity or low-rank priors, via FAVOR+; the abstract describes the guarantees as unbiased or nearly-unbiased estimation of the attention matrix, uniform convergence and low estimation variance.([Abstract, p.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2009.14794v4#page=1 "We introduce Performers, Transformer architectures which can estimate regular (softmax) full-rank-attention Transformers with provable accuracy, but using only linear (as opposed to quadratic) space and time complexity, without relying on any priors such as sparsity or low-rankness."))
- **c2** Generalized kernelizable attention: for A(i, j) = K(q_i, k_j) with K(x, y) = E[phi(x)^T phi(y)] and random feature map phi: R^d -> R^r_+, the attention is approximated as D^-1(Q'((K')^T V)), with space O(Lr + Ld + rd) and time O(Lrd) instead of O(L^2 + Ld) and O(L^2 d) for regular attention.([Section 2.2, p.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2009.14794v4#page=3 "It is easy to see that such a mechanism is characterized by space complexity O(Lr + Ld + rd) and time complexity O(Lrd)"))
- **c3** Why trigonometric random features fail: the unbiased sin/cos random-feature estimator of the softmax kernel can give negative values, and kernel scores close to 0 (many entries of A for low-relevance tokens) are estimated with large variance; this leads to abnormal behaviour such as negative renormalizers D^-1, which either completely prevents training or leads to sub-optimal models.([Section 2.3, p.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2009.14794v4#page=4 "Applying random feature maps with potentially negative dimension-values (sin / cos) leads to unstable behaviours"))
- **c4** Lemma 1 (positive random features): SM(x, y) = E_{w~N(0,I_d)}[exp(w^T x - ||x||^2/2) exp(w^T y - ||y||^2/2)], so the softmax kernel admits an unbiased positive random-feature approximation (and a hyperbolic variant with f1 = exp(u), f2 = exp(-u) for further variance reduction).([Section 2.3, p.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2009.14794v4#page=4 "Consequently, softmax-kernel admits a positive random feature map unbiased approximation with"))
- **c5** Variance results: by Lemma 2 (independent samples), as SM(x, y) -> 0 the MSE of the trigonometric estimator goes to infinity while that of the positive estimator goes to 0; Theorem 2 states that orthogonal random features (which require m <= d) provably reduce the MSE of the positive estimator for any d > 0, with an explicit gap.([Section 3, p.5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2009.14794v4#page=5 "Our next result shows that orthogonality provably reduces mean squared error of the estimation with positive random features for any dimensionality d > 0 and we explicitly provide the gap."))
- **c6** Theorem 4 (uniform convergence) is proved for the trigonometric estimator with ORFs under the assumption that query/key L2-norms are bounded by R: the number of random projections m needed for ||A_hat - A||_inf <= epsilon with constant probability depends on d, R and epsilon but not on the sequence length L.([Section 3, p.6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2009.14794v4#page=6 "but does not depend on input sequence length L"))
- **c7** Speed and memory are measured for the backward pass in the bidirectional setting with the default size (8 heads, 6 layers, d_ff 2048, d 512) on a V100 GPU with 16GB, compared against an 'X' line where attention simply returns V (the maximum possible speedup); the Performer reaches nearly linear time and sub-quadratic memory in L.([Section 4.1, p.7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2009.14794v4#page=7 "We observed (Fig. 3) that in terms of L, the Performer reaches nearly linear time and sub-quadratic memory consumption"))
- **c8** Caveat on approximating pretrained softmax models: small attention approximation errors can propagate through multiple layers, so very tight approximations may sometimes be needed; transferring pretrained Transformer weights requires small finetuning, and on PG-19 positive softmax features with redrawing were needed to match regular Transformers, while trigonometric features became highly unstable.([Section 4.3, p.7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2009.14794v4#page=7 "In other words, the model’s Lipschitz constant can easily scale up small attention approximation error, which means that very tight approximations may sometimes be needed."))
