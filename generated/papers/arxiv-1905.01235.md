## 主張

出典のリンクは原論文PDFの該当ページを開く。リンクにカーソルを合わせると原文の引用が表示される。

- **c1** The benchmark is based on the principle that a good representation transfers to many different tasks and does so with limited supervision and limited fine-tuning; the authors view self-supervised learning as learning features rather than as an initialization method, so they perform limited fine-tuning.([Introduction, p.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1905.01235v2#page=2 "Our benchmark is based on the following principle: a good representation (1) transfers to many different tasks, and, (2) transfers with limited supervision and limited ﬁne-tuning."))
- **c2** The authors report inconsistencies across previous methods' evaluations (image crops, weight re-scaling, pre-processing, longer fine-tuning schedules) that affect final performance, and argue that a consistent setup with hyperparameters set fairly for all methods is needed to separate better representations from better transfer optimization.([Section 6 (footnote 1), p.5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1905.01235v2#page=5 "We discovered inconsistencies across previous methods (different image crops for evaluation, weights re-scaling, pre-processing, longer ﬁne-tuning schedules etc.) which affects the ﬁnal performance."))
- **c3** Linear-classification protocol for VOC07/COCO2014: one linear SVM per class on frozen, L2-normalized features (from several layers: after every conv layer for AlexNet, the last layer of every residual stage for ResNet-50), with the cost chosen per class by 3-fold cross-validation over 26 values on the training data; Places205 uses linear classifiers trained with SGD following Zhang et al.([Appendix D.1, p.14](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1905.01235v2#page=14 "We use 3-fold cross-validation to choose the cost parameter per class and then further calculate the mean average precision."))
- **c4** The authors found that SGD-trained linear classifiers, although common practice, can give significantly different results depending on optimization hyperparameters (number of iterations, learning-rate decay) because of slow convergence; they obtained more robust results with dual coordinate descent (linear SVM, LIBLINEAR) and use it for VOC07 and COCO2014.([Appendix D.6, p.15](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1905.01235v2#page=15 "Although, using SGD based Linear Classiﬁers is a common practice [80] to evaluate representations, we found that optimization hyperparameters can lead to signﬁcantly different results."))
- **c5** Pre-training domain matters: for Jigsaw, ImageNet-22k pre-training transfers to VOC07 as well as YFCC-100M, while on Places205 YFCC-1M transfers as well as ImageNet-22k; a similar trend holds for Colorization. A possible explanation offered is that ImageNet is closer to VOC07 (object-centric) and YFCC to Places205 (scene-centric), which motivates evaluating on varied domains and tasks.([Section 5, p.5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1905.01235v2#page=5 "A possible explanation for this beneﬁt is that the domain (image distribution) of ImageNet is closer to VOC07 (both are object-centric) whereas YFCC is closer to Places205 (both are scene-centric)."))
- **c6** Low-shot linear SVM evaluation (k positives per class, 5 independent training samples, ResNet-50 best layer res4): a significant gap between supervised and self-supervised features remains, larger at small sample sizes (stated as 30 points at k=1 versus 20 points full-shot on Places205); for lower layers the self-supervised features are competitive.([Section 6.2, p.6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1905.01235v2#page=6 "This gap is much larger at lower sample size, e.g., at k=1 it is 30 points for Places205, whereas at higher values (full-shot in Table 3) it is 20 points."))
- **c7** Full fine-tuning is excluded from the benchmark suite (reported only as a legacy task) because, the authors argue, it evaluates not only the representation but also the initialization and optimization method.([Section 7, p.8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1905.01235v2#page=8 "We argue that this view evaluates not only the quality of the representations but also the initialization and optimization method."))
- **c8** ImageNet-1k linear classification is excluded from the benchmark suite: evaluating on ImageNet-1k does not test generalization of the supervised baseline trained on it, and most self-supervised methods pre-train on ImageNet-1k, so pre-training and evaluating in the same domain (here on identical images) biases evaluation; results are still reported for comparison.([Section 7, p.8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1905.01235v2#page=8 "Hence, evaluating also on the same dataset (ImageNet-1k) does not test generalization of the supervised baseline."))
