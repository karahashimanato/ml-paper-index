## 主張

出典のリンクは原論文PDFの該当ページを開く。リンクにカーソルを合わせると原文の引用が表示される。

- **c1** Design: the stated primary goal is an apples-to-apples comparison of Mamba, Mamba-2, Mamba-2-Hybrid and Transformers at 8B parameters trained on up to 3.5T tokens with the same hyperparameters, the same data and the exact same evaluation setup on 35 downstream tasks; the authors note that earlier controlled comparisons (same data, parameter count) were limited to small scale.([Introduction, p.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2406.07887v1#page=2 "In particular, our primary goal is to provide a rigorous apples-to-apples comparison between Mamba, Mamba-2, Mamba-2-Hybrid (containing Mamba-2, attention, and MLP layers), and Transformers for 8B-parameter models trained on up to 3.5T tokens, with the same hyperparameters."))
- **c2** Hyperparameters are shared across architectures because smaller-scale studies found Mamba hyperparameters similar to Transformers' (1.1T: batch 256, peak LR 1e-4; 3.5T: batch 1024, peak LR 3e-4; cosine schedule, BF16).([Section 3.2, p.5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2406.07887v1#page=5 "We performed some studies at smaller scale and found that Mamba network hyperparameters are similar to that of Transformers and as a result we use the same hyperparameters across models to make a rigorous direct comparison."))
- **c3** At 1.1T tokens, pure SSMs average higher than the Transformer on WinoGrande, PIQA, HellaSwag, ARC-Easy and ARC-Challenge but are significantly worse on MMLU (Mamba-2 five-shot 17 points lower); training on 3.5T tokens closes the Mamba-2 MMLU gap to 1.37 points.([Section 3.3.1, p.6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2406.07887v1#page=6 "For example Mamba-2 five-shot accuracy is 17 points lower than that of the Transformer in this setting."))
- **c4** MMLU formats: pure SSMs struggle with the standard and choice-text-in-targets formulations but exceed the Transformer in the cloze formulation; the authors read this as SSMs containing the same knowledge but requiring substantially more training to understand the multiple-choice format, and hypothesize that pure SSMs cannot directly route each answer's knowledge into a single answer token.([Section 3.3.2, p.6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2406.07887v1#page=6 "While the pure SSM models struggle with the standard and choice-text-in-targets formulations, they actually exceed the accuracy of the Transformer in the cloze setting."))
- **c5** Copying (Phonebook, averaged over 20 random initializations): the 8B Transformer answers with near 100% accuracy up to its 4096 pretraining context, whereas Mamba and Mamba-2 start answering incorrectly beyond roughly 500 tokens, which persists for Mamba-2 at 3.5T tokens; SSMs show 'fuzzy memory' (similar but not exact numbers), and a reversed prompt helps but accuracy still degrades below 4096; the authors hypothesize fine-tuning on the task would help.([Section 3.3.3, p.8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2406.07887v1#page=8 "In contrast, both Mamba and Mamba-2 models begin to respond incorrectly for input sequence lengths beyond approximately 500 tokens."))
- **c6** Hybrid architecture: out of 56 layers, 4 (7.1%) are self-attention (GQA), 24 (42.9%) Mamba-2 and 28 (50%) MLP, with a Mamba-2 layer first, attention and MLP layers evenly distributed, no explicit position embeddings, and the exact same training hyperparameters as the Transformer and pure SSM models; Mamba-2 is chosen over Mamba citing an up to 8x faster scan (Dao and Gu 2024).([Section 4.2, p.10](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2406.07887v1#page=10 "Out of 56 total layers, the hybrid model has 4 (7.1%) self-attention layers, 24 (42.9%) Mamba-2 layers, and 28 (50%) MLP layers."))
- **c7** Training efficiency is measured as Model FLOP Utilization on 1024 NVIDIA H100 GPUs (tensor-parallel 4, data-parallel 256): the hybrid reaches 29.9% MFU versus 30.7% for a corresponding 8B Transformer in Megatron-LM with the same parallelization.([Section 4.2, p.11](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2406.07887v1#page=11 "This can be compared to the 30.7% MFU of a corresponding 8B parameter Transformer implemented in Megatron-LM and trained with the same parallelization configuration."))
- **c8** Inference speed is a predicted (not described as measured) per-token generation time ratio at batch size 32: roughly equal for short contexts and nearly 8x faster for the hybrid at long contexts; the authors additionally expect benefits from a smaller key-value cache allowing larger batches.([Section 4.2, p.11](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2406.07887v1#page=11 "In Figure 5, we plot the predicted time to generate one token for the 8B Transformer model over the time for the 8B Mamba-2-Hybrid model using a batch size of 32."))
- **c9** Long-context weakness: 16K/32K Transformers reach about one point higher average than the hybrids on natural long-context tasks, largely on LongBench multi-document QA; the authors hypothesize SSM states are sometimes confused by irrelevant documents, possibly due to the continued-pretraining recipe packing unrelated sequences, and note hybrids may be more sensitive to prompt formatting (prompts were optimized for Transformers).([Section 4.3.2, p.12](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2406.07887v1#page=12 "We hypothesize that the hybrid model reaches lower accuracy than the Transformer on these tasks because the SSM layer states are sometimes confused by documents irrelevant to the question"))
