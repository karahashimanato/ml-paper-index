## 1. 定式化の系譜

### 出発点: 構造を壊す過程と、それを戻す過程

Sohl-Dickstein らは、データの構造を順方向の拡散でゆっくり壊し、それを戻す逆方向の拡散を学習する、という生成モデルを提案した [arxiv-1503.03585#c1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1503.03585v8#page=1 "The essential idea, inspired by non-equilibrium statistical physics, is to systematically and slowly destroy structure in a data distribution through an iterative forward diffusion process.")。
逆方向の各段がガウス分布で書ける根拠は、先行研究を引いた次の性質である。拡散の1段が十分小さければ、逆過程も順過程と同じ関数形になる [arxiv-1503.03585#c2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1503.03585v8#page=5 "For both Gaussian and binomial diffusion, for continuous diffusion (limit of small step size β) the reversal of the diffusion process has the identical functional form as the forward process (Feller, 1949).")。
代償として、生成には段数の数だけネットワークを評価する必要がある [arxiv-1503.03585#c3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1503.03585v8#page=5 "The computational cost of running this algorithm is the cost of these functions, times the number of time-steps.")。学習は対数尤度の下界の最大化で、密度推定を回帰の問題に変える [arxiv-1503.03585#c4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1503.03585v8#page=6 "Thus, the task of estimating a probability distribution has been reduced to the task of performing regression on the functions which set the mean and covariance of a sequence of Gaussians (or set the state ﬂip probability for a sequence of Bernoulli trials).")。

### DDPM: ノイズを予測する

Ho らは、加えたノイズ ε をネットワークに予測させる形にすると、学習は複数のノイズの強さでのノイズ除去スコアマッチングと、生成はアニーリングしたランジュバン動力学と同等になることを示した [arxiv-2006.11239#c1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2006.11239v2#page=2 "We obtained our best sample quality results using this parameterization (Section 4.2), so we consider this equivalence to be one of our primary contributions.") [arxiv-2006.11239#c3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2006.11239v2#page=4 "We have shown that the ϵ-prediction parameterization both resembles Langevin dynamics and simpliﬁes the diffusion model’s variational bound to an objective that resembles denoising score matching.")。

- 順方向のノイズの量は学習せず定数に固定する [arxiv-2006.11239#c2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2006.11239v2#page=3 "Thus, in our implementation, the approximate posterior q has no learnable parameters, so LT is a constant during training and can be ignored.")。
- 下界の各項の重みを落とした簡略化した損失は、ノイズの小さい段を軽く扱い、難しい段に集中させる [arxiv-2006.11239#c4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2006.11239v2#page=5 "Since our simpliﬁed objective (14) discards the weighting in Eq. (12), it is a weighted variational bound that emphasizes different aspects of reconstruction compared to the standard variational bound [18, 22].")。

### スコアベースの見方

Song と Ermon は、データ分布の対数密度の勾配(スコア)を推定して、ランジュバン動力学でサンプルを作る方法を扱った。素朴なスコアの推定には3つの問題がある。

- データが低次元の多様体上にあると、スコアが定義できない [arxiv-1907.05600#c1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1907.05600v3#page=2 "First, if the data distribution is supported on a low dimensional manifold—as it is often assumed for many real world datasets—the score will be undeﬁned in the ambient space, and score matching will fail to provide a consistent score estimator.")。
- 密度の低い領域では推定が不正確になる [arxiv-1907.05600#c2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1907.05600v3#page=4 "As the ﬁgure demonstrates, score estimation is only reliable in the immediate vicinity of the modes of pdata, where the data density is high.")。
- 離れた山の間をランジュバン動力学が行き来できない [arxiv-1907.05600#c3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1907.05600v3#page=4 "In this case, Langevin dynamics can produce correct samples in theory, but may require a very small step size and a very large number of steps to mix.")。

複数の強さのノイズを加えてスコアを学習することで、これに対処した [arxiv-1907.05600#c5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1907.05600v3#page=6 "We adopt denoising score matching as it is slightly faster and naturally ﬁts the task of estimating scores of noise-perturbed data distributions.")。単一の小さいノイズでは、まともな画像を生成できなかった [arxiv-1907.05600#c7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1907.05600v3#page=8 "As a result, this baseline fails to generate reasonable images, as shown by samples in Appendix C.1.")。

この話は、オートエンコーダの記事で扱った「ノイズ除去オートエンコーダはスコアを学ぶ」という結果 [arxiv-1211.4246#c1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1211.4246v5#page=1 "We show that the auto-encoder captures the score (derivative of the log-density with respect to the input).") と同じ系譜にある(本記事の整理)。

### SDE による統一

Song らは、ノイズを加える過程を確率微分方程式(SDE)で書いた。その逆時間の SDE は、各時刻のスコアだけに依存する [arxiv-2011.13456#c1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2011.13456v2#page=1 "Crucially, the reverse-time SDE depends only on the time-dependent gradient ﬁeld (a.k.a., score) of the perturbed data distribution.")。

- ノイズの段数を無限にすると、スコアベースの方式は分散が発散する SDE(VE)、DDPM は分散を保つ SDE(VP)になる [arxiv-2011.13456#c3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2011.13456v2#page=5 "Therefore, the noise perturbations used in SMLD and DDPM correspond to discretizations of SDEs Eqs. (9) and (11).")。DDPM の目的関数は、各ノイズの強さでのスコアを暗に計算している [arxiv-2011.13456#c2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2011.13456v2#page=1 "For continuous state spaces, the DDPM training objective implicitly computes scores at each noise scale.")。
- 同じ周辺分布を持つ決定的な常微分方程式(probability flow ODE)を使うと、尤度を厳密に計算できる [arxiv-2011.13456#c5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2011.13456v2#page=7 "We compute log-likelihoods on uniformly dequantized data, and only compare to models evaluated in the same way (omitting models evaluated with variational dequantization (Ho et al., 2019) or discrete data)")。
- 限界として、サンプリングは GAN より遅く、サンプラーの選択肢が多くのハイパーパラメータを持ち込む [arxiv-2011.13456#c8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2011.13456v2#page=9 "While our proposed sampling approaches improve results and enable more efﬁcient sampling, they remain slower at sampling than GANs (Goodfellow et al., 2014) on the same datasets.")。

## 2. 尤度とサンプルの質、VAE とのつながり

### 尤度とサンプルの質は一致しない

拡散モデルの研究では、尤度の良さとサンプルの質の良さが一致しないことが繰り返し報告されている。

- DDPM: 下界そのもので学習すると符号長(尤度)が良くなり、簡略化した損失で学習するとサンプルの質が最も良かった [arxiv-2006.11239#c5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2006.11239v2#page=6 "We ﬁnd that training our models on the true variational bound yields better codelengths than training on the simpliﬁed objective, as expected, but the latter yields the best sample quality.")。サンプルの質は高いのに、対数尤度は他の尤度ベースのモデルに及ばなかった [arxiv-2006.11239#c6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2006.11239v2#page=2 "Despite their sample quality, our models do not have competitive log likelihoods compared to other likelihood-based models")。
- Improved DDPM: 尤度が劣る理由を調べ、変分下界には最初の数段の寄与が大きいことを見つけた [arxiv-2102.09672#c1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.09672v1#page=3 "Thus, it is important to explore why DDPMs seem to perform poorly on this metric, since this may suggest a fundamental shortcoming such as bad mode coverage.") [arxiv-2102.09672#c2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.09672v1#page=3 "Thus, it seems likely that we could improve log-likelihood by using a better choice of Σθ(xt, t).")。下界を直接最適化すると尤度はさらに良くなるが、FID は悪化した [arxiv-2102.09672#c5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.09672v1#page=5 "Optimizing Lvlb further improves log-likelihood at the cost of a higher FID.")。
- SDE: VE はサンプルの質が良く、VP は尤度が良い傾向があり、領域ごとに試す必要がありそうだ、と著者らは述べる [arxiv-2011.13456#c6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2011.13456v2#page=8 "As shown in Table 3, VE SDEs typically provide better sample quality than VP/sub-VP SDEs, but we also empirically observe that their likelihoods are worse than VP/sub-VP SDE counterparts.")。
- VDM: 尤度向けに調整したモデルは、FID を目標にした拡散モデルより FID が劣った [arxiv-2107.00630#c5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2107.00630v6#page=8 "Our CIFAR-10 model, whose hyper-parameters were tuned for likelihood, results in a FID (perceptual quality) score of 7.41.")。著者らは、DDPM 系の FID が良いのは損失の重みが大域的な構造を重視するためだ、という仮説を述べている [arxiv-2107.00630#c7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2107.00630v6#page=21 "The latter two works report better FID and Inception Score than Song et al. [2020] and the current paper, which we hypothesize is due to their loss emphasizing the global consistence and coarse level patterns more than the ﬁne scale features of the data.")。

### VAE とのつながり

- VDM は、拡散モデルを VAE の一種とみなす [arxiv-2107.00630#c1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2107.00630v6#page=2 "DPMs can be viewed as a type of variational autoencoder (VAE) [Kingma and Welling, 2013, Rezende et al., 2014], whose structure and loss function allows for efﬁcient training of arbitrarily deep models.")。連続時間では、下界はノイズのスケジュールの形によらず、両端の信号対雑音比(SNR)だけで決まる [arxiv-2107.00630#c2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2107.00630v6#page=6 "The VLB is thus only impacted by the function SNR(t) through its endpoints SNRmin and SNRmax.")。
- チュートリアルとサーベイも、拡散モデルを「エンコーダが固定の線形ガウス変換で、潜在の次元がデータと同じ」階層的 VAE として説明している [arxiv-2208.11970#c1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2208.11970v1#page=6 "The easiest way to think of a Variational Diﬀusion Model (VDM) [4, 5, 6] is simply as a Markovian Hierarchical Variational Autoencoder with three key restrictions:") [arxiv-2209.00796#c6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2209.00796v15#page=21 "The DDPM can be conceptualized as a hierarchical Markovian VAE with a fixed encoder.")。
- その帰結として、潜在表現に意味がなく、次元もデータと同じで、サンプリングが高価になる [arxiv-2208.11970#c7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2208.11970v1#page=22 "The VDM does not produce interpretable latents.") [arxiv-2209.00796#c8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2209.00796v15#page=44 "Unlike variational autoencoders or generative adversarial networks, diffusion models are less effective for providing good representations of data in their latent space.")。

### 評価を読むときの注意(尤度)

- **量子化への過学習**: Sohl-Dickstein らの初期の版は、8ビットの画素の量子化をモデルが学んだことで、CIFAR-10 の尤度の下界が高く出ていた [arxiv-1503.03585#c7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1503.03585v8#page=7 "These were the result of the model learning the 8-bit quantization of pixel values in the CIFAR-10 dataset.")。厳密な尤度は一様ノイズで量子化を消したデータで計算し、同じ方法で評価したモデルとだけ比べる必要がある [arxiv-2011.13456#c5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2011.13456v2#page=7 "We compute log-likelihoods on uniformly dequantized data, and only compare to models evaluated in the same way (omitting models evaluated with variational dequantization (Ho et al., 2019) or discrete data)")。
- **Parzen 窓での比較**: MNIST の比較は、先行研究に合わせて Parzen 窓の推定で行われた [arxiv-1503.03585#c8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1503.03585v8#page=9 "For this comparison we therefore estimate MNIST log likelihood using the Parzen-window code released with (Goodfellow et al., 2014).")。オートエンコーダの記事で見たとおり、この指標には欠陥が指摘されている。

## 3. サンプリングの高速化

拡散モデルの最大の実用上の弱点は、生成に何百〜何千回もネットワークを評価することである [arxiv-2006.11239#c7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2006.11239v2#page=5 "We set T = 1000 for all experiments so that the number of neural network evaluations needed during sampling matches previous work [53, 55].")。高速化には、**学習し直さない方法**と、**蒸留で新しいモデルを学習する方法**がある。

| 方法 | 学習し直しが要るか | 仕組み | 代償・限界 |
|---|---|---|---|
| DDIM | 不要。DDPM と同じ学習で、学習済みモデルをそのまま使える [arxiv-2010.02502#c2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2010.02502v4#page=4 "Therefore, we can essentially use pretrained DDPM models as the solutions to the new objectives, and focus on ﬁnding a generative process that is better at producing samples subject to our needs by changing σ.") | 非マルコフの順過程を考え、段の部分列だけで生成する [arxiv-2010.02502#c3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2010.02502v4#page=5 "which accelerates the corresponding generative processes without having to train a different model.") | 段数を減らすほど質は下がる [arxiv-2010.02502#c7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2010.02502v4#page=7 "As expected, the sample quality becomes higher as we increase dim(τ), presenting a trade-off between sample quality and computational costs.") |
| DPM-Solver | 不要 [arxiv-2206.00927#c2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2206.00927v3#page=1 "DPM-Solver is suitable for both discrete-time and continuous-time DPMs without any further training.") | 拡散の ODE の線形部分を解析的に解く [arxiv-2206.00927#c1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2206.00927v3#page=1 "The formulation analytically computes the linear part of the solution, rather than leaving all terms to black-box ODE solvers as adopted in previous works.")。DDIM はその1次の場合と一致する [arxiv-2206.00927#c5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2206.00927v3#page=7 "Although motivated by entirely different perspectives, we show that the updates of DPM-Solver-1 and Denoising Diffusion Implicit Models (DDIM) [19] are identical.") | 尤度の評価の高速化には向かない。GAN ほど速くはない [arxiv-2206.00927#c9](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2206.00927v3#page=10 "Besides, compared to the commonly-used GANs, diffusion models with DPM-Solver are still not fast enough for real-time applications.") |
| 段階的な蒸留 | 必要(教師モデル) | 生徒の1段が教師の2段に一致するよう学習し、これを繰り返す [arxiv-2202.00512#c1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2202.00512v2#page=3 "At every iteration of progressive distillation, we then initialize the student model with a copy of the teacher, using both the same parameters and same model deﬁnition.") | 段数が非常に少ないと質が落ちやすい [arxiv-2202.00512#c6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2202.00512v2#page=7 "At 2 or 1 sampling steps, the sample quality degrades relatively more quickly.")。生徒は教師と同じ構造 [arxiv-2202.00512#c9](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2202.00512v2#page=9 "In the current work we limited ourselves to setups where the student model has the same architecture and number of parameters as the teacher model:") |
| Consistency models | 蒸留版は教師が必要、学習版は不要 [arxiv-2303.01469#c2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2303.01469v2#page=2 "By contrast, our second method eliminates the need for a pre-trained diffusion model altogether, allowing us to train a consistency model in isolation.") | ノイズからデータへ直接写す。1段でも生成でき、段数を増やすと質が上がる [arxiv-2303.01469#c1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2303.01469v2#page=1 "They support fast one-step generation by design, while still al-lowing multistep sampling to trade compute for sample quality.") | 学習版は離散化の段数に非常に敏感 [arxiv-2303.01469#c6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2303.01469v2#page=7 "As demonstrated in Fig. 3d, the con-vergence of CT is highly sensitive to N—smaller N leads to faster convergence but worse samples, whereas larger N leads to slower convergence but better samples upon convergence.") |

学習し直さない方法を推す側は、蒸留は学習の手間がかかり、元のモデルの情報の一部を失うと指摘する [arxiv-2206.00927#c3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2206.00927v3#page=2 "Such methods require a possibly expensive training stage before they can be used for efﬁcient sampling.") [arxiv-2206.00927#c6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2206.00927v3#page=8 "Although the progressive distillation method [13] can obtain a fast sampler within 4 steps, it needs further training costs and loses part of the information in the original DPM")。蒸留を推す側は、先行の蒸留法は元のモデルで大量のサンプルを事前に作る必要があったが、段階的な蒸留は元のモデルを全段数で走らせる必要がない、と述べる [arxiv-2202.00512#c8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2202.00512v2#page=8 "In contrast, our method never needs to run the original model at the full number of sampling steps:")。

### 評価を読むときの注意(高速化)

- **設定を評価指標で選んでいる**: DDIM は、段の部分列の取り方を FID で選んでいる [arxiv-2010.02502#c6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2010.02502v4#page=18 "These choices achieve slightly better FID than their alternatives in the respective datasets.")。consistency models の多段生成の時刻は、FID を最適化する探索で決めている [arxiv-2303.01469#c4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2303.01469v2#page=4 "where the time points are pinpointed one at a time using ternary search to optimize the FID of samples obtained from Algorithm 1.")。段階的な蒸留の表は、学習中の最良の値を報告している [arxiv-2202.00512#c4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2202.00512v2#page=6 "All reported results are averages over 3 random seeds of the best metrics obtained over 2 million training steps; nevertheless we ﬁnd results are still ±0.1 due to the noise inherent in training our models.")。
- **ベースラインの扱い**: 段階的な蒸留の比較では、確率的サンプラーのパラメータを段数ごとに調整し、最良の結果だけを示している [arxiv-2202.00512#c7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2202.00512v2#page=8 "We then tune this interpolation coefﬁcient separately for each number of sampling steps and report only the best result for that number of steps:")。consistency models の比較では、段階的な蒸留を、改善の分かっている設定ではなく元の設定で報告している [arxiv-2303.01469#c8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2303.01469v2#page=7 "All methods distill from an EDM (Karras et al., 2022) model that we pre-trained in-house.")。
- **費用は「ネットワークの評価回数」で測る**: DPM-Solver や EDM は、評価回数(NFE)を軸に比べている [arxiv-2206.00927#c7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2206.00927v3#page=8 "For each experiment, We draw 50K samples and use the widely adopted FID score [37] to evaluate the sample quality, where lower FID usually implies better sample quality.") [arxiv-2206.00364#c2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2206.00364v2#page=6 "Together, they reduce the NFE needed to reach high-quality results by a large factor: 7.3× for VP, 300× for VE, and 3.2× for DDIM, corresponding to the highlighted NFE values in Figure 2.")。

## 4. 条件付け、潜在空間、設計の選択

### ガイダンス — 多様性と忠実さの引き換え

- **分類器ガイダンス**: ノイズの入った画像で学習した分類器の勾配で、生成を条件付ける [arxiv-2105.05233#c3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.05233v4#page=8 "When using a scale of 1, we observed that the classiﬁer assigned reasonable probabilities (around 50%) to the desired classes for the ﬁnal samples, but these samples did not match the intended classes upon visual inspection.")。導出には、分類器の対数尤度の曲率が小さいという近似を使う [arxiv-2105.05233#c2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.05233v4#page=7 "This assumption is reasonable in the limit of inﬁnite diffusion steps, where //Σ// →0.")。勾配の重み(スケール)を上げると、多様性(再現率)を犠牲に忠実さ(精度、IS)が上がる [arxiv-2105.05233#c5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.05233v4#page=9 "We see that scaling the gradients beyond 1.0 smoothly trades off recall (a measure of diversity) for higher precision and IS (measures of ﬁdelity).")。これはクラスの分布を鋭くすることと同じである [arxiv-2105.05233#c4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.05233v4#page=8 "In other words, using a larger gradient scale focuses more on the modes of the classiﬁer, which is potentially desirable for producing higher ﬁdelity (but less diverse) samples.")。ラベル付きのデータにしか使えない [arxiv-2105.05233#c10](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.05233v4#page=12 "Our proposed classiﬁer guidance technique is currently limited to labeled datasets, and we have provided no effective strategy for trading off diversity for ﬁdelity on unlabeled datasets.")。
- **分類器なしガイダンス**: 条件付きと条件なしのモデルを1つのネットワークで同時に学習し、その差で導く [arxiv-2207.12598#c2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2207.12598v1#page=5 "(It would certainly be possible to train separate models instead of jointly training them together, but we choose joint training because it is extremely simple to implement, does not complicate the training pipeline, and does not increase the total number of parameters.)")。追加の分類器が要らないかわりに、各段でネットワークを2回評価する [arxiv-2207.12598#c8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2207.12598v1#page=8 "Because we used the same model architecture as ADM-G, the fair comparison in terms of sampling speed would be our T = 128 setting, which underperforms compared to ADM-G in terms of FID score.")。FID はガイダンスが弱いとき、IS は強いときに最良になり、その間でトレードオフがある [arxiv-2207.12598#c6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2207.12598v1#page=7 "We obtain the best FID results with a small amount of guidance (w = 0.1 or w = 0.3, depending on the dataset) and the best IS result with strong guidance (w ≥4).")。著者らは、多様性が下がることの悪影響に懸念を示している [arxiv-2207.12598#c9](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2207.12598v1#page=9 "There may be negative impacts in deployed models, since sample diversity is important to maintain in applications where certain parts of the data are underrepresented in the context of the rest of the data.")。

### 潜在空間での拡散

Rombach らは、事前に学習したオートエンコーダの潜在空間で拡散を行う。画素空間の強力な拡散モデルは学習に数百 GPU 日を要する(先行研究を引いた記述)ことが動機である [arxiv-2112.10752#c1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2112.10752v2#page=1 "As an example, training the most powerful DMs often takes hundreds of GPU days (e.g. 150 - 1000 V100 days in [15]) and repeated evaluations on a noisy version of the input space render also inference expensive,")。

- オートエンコーダの潜在には、軽い KL の罰則か、ベクトル量子化をかける [arxiv-2112.10752#c2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2112.10752v2#page=4 "The ﬁrst variant, KL-reg., imposes a slight KL-penalty towards a standard normal on the learned latent, similar to a VAE [46, 69], whereas VQ-reg. uses a vector quantization layer [96] within the decoder.")。量子化の方が再構成はやや悪いのに、生成の質は良いことがあった [arxiv-2112.10752#c3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2112.10752v2#page=5 "Interestingly, we ﬁnd that LDMs trained in VQ-regularized latent spaces sometimes achieve better sample quality, even though the reconstruction capabilities of VQ-regularized ﬁrst stage models slightly fall behind those of their continuous counterparts, cf. Tab. 8.")。
- 圧縮率にはトレードオフがある。圧縮が弱すぎると学習が遅く、強すぎると質が頭打ちになる [arxiv-2112.10752#c4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2112.10752v2#page=5 "we attribute this to i) leaving most of perceptual compression to the diffusion model and ii) too strong ﬁrst stage compression resulting in information loss and thus limiting the achievable quality.")。
- オートエンコーダの再構成の精度が、細かい画素の正確さを要するタスクではボトルネックになりうる [arxiv-2112.10752#c9](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2112.10752v2#page=9 "Moreover, the use of LDMs can be questionable when high precision is required: although the loss of image quality is very small in our f = 4 autoencoding models (see Fig. 1), their reconstruction capability can become a bottleneck for tasks that require ﬁne-grained accuracy in pixel space.")。

### 設計の選択(EDM)

Karras らは、拡散モデルの設計を、サンプラー・ネットワークの前処理・学習の各部分に分けて検討した。サンプラーの選択は学習とほぼ独立だという仮説を、3つの学習済みモデルで確かめている [arxiv-2206.00364#c1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2206.00364v2#page=4 "Our hypothesis is that the choices related to the sampling process are largely independent of the other components, such as network architecture and training details.")。

- 確率的なサンプリングは、細部の喪失や色のずれを招く [arxiv-2206.00364#c3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2206.00364v2#page=7 "We suspect that practical denoisers induce a slightly non-conservative vector ﬁeld in Eq. 3, violating the premises of Langevin diffusion and causing these detrimental effects.")。自分たちの CIFAR-10 の設定では決定的なサンプリングが最良で、ImageNet-64 では確率的な方が明らかに良かった [arxiv-2206.00364#c6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2206.00364v2#page=9 "When using our training setup in CIFAR-10 (Figure 5b), the best results were obtained with deterministic sampling, and any amount of stochastic sampling was detrimental.")。
- 確率的なサンプラーのパラメータは個別にグリッド探索する必要があり、それを主な評価に使うと設計の比較を偏らせうる、と著者ら自身が懸念している [arxiv-2206.00364#c4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2206.00364v2#page=8 "This raises a general concern that using stochastic sampling as the primary means of evaluating model improvements may inadvertently end up inﬂuencing the design choices related to model architecture and training.")。

### 評価を読むときの注意(条件付け・設計)

- **ガイダンスの強さの選び方**: 分類器ガイダンスの「最適な」スケールは小さなグリッドから選ばれ、どの指標・どのデータで選んだかは本文に書かれていない [arxiv-2105.05233#c8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.05233v4#page=27 "When choosing optimal classiﬁer scales for our sampler, we swept over [0.5, 1, 2] for ImageNet 128×128 and ImageNet 256×256, and [1, 2, 3, 3.5, 4, 4.5, 5] for ImageNet 512×512.")。分類器なしガイダンスでも、最良の強さは報告と同じ掃引から来ている [arxiv-2207.12598#c5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2207.12598v1#page=7 "calculate FID and Inception Scores with 50000 samples for each value following the procedures of Heusel et al. (2017) and Salimans et al. (2016)")。潜在拡散のスケールの選び方は記述が見当たらない [arxiv-2112.10752#c7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2112.10752v2#page=7 "We note that applying classiﬁer-free diffusion guidance [32] greatly boosts sample quality, such that the guided LDM-KL-8-G is on par with the recent state-of-the-art AR [26] and diffusion models [59] for text-to-image synthesis, while substantially reducing parameter count.")。
- **チェックポイントの選び方**: EDM は FID が最小の時点を報告している [arxiv-2206.00364#c8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2206.00364v2#page=45 "We saved a snapshot of the model every 2.5 million images and reported results for the snapshot that achieved the lowest FID according to our deterministic sampler with NFE = 35 or NFE = 79, depending on the resolution.")。DDPM、NCSN、SDE の論文も、学習中で FID が最小のチェックポイントを報告している [arxiv-2006.11239#c8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2006.11239v2#page=15 "Sample quality scores and log likelihood are reported on the minimum FID value over the course of training.") [arxiv-1907.05600#c8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1907.05600v3#page=15 "For selecting our CIFAR-10 and CelebA models, we generate 1000 images for each checkpoint and choose the one with the smallest FID score computed on these 1000 images.") [arxiv-2011.13456#c7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2011.13456v2#page=8 "Results reported in Table 3 are for the checkpoint with the smallest FID over the course of training, where samples are generated with PC samplers.")。
- **計算量の比較**: 異なるハードウェアの計算量を、換算係数を仮定して比べている [arxiv-2105.05233#c9](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.05233v4#page=18 "We note that our classiﬁer training is relatively lightweight compared to training the generative model.") [arxiv-2112.10752#c8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2112.10752v2#page=28 "we convert the A100 days to V100 days by assuming a ×2.2 speedup of A100 vs V100")。

## 5. 評価指標と、学習データの記憶

### FID の前提と弱点

FID は、生成画像と実画像を Inception ネットワークの特徴に通し、その平均と共分散だけを比べる指標である。特徴がガウス分布に従うと仮定している [arxiv-1706.08500#c3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1706.08500v6#page=6 "The Gaussian is the maximum entropy distribution for given mean and covariance, therefore we assume the coding units to follow a multidimensional Gaussian.")。Inception Score が実画像の統計を使わないことへの対案として提案された [arxiv-1706.08500#c2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1706.08500v6#page=6 "Drawback of the Inception Score is that the statistics of real world samples are not used and compared to the statistics of synthetic samples.")。根拠として示されたのは、6種類の人工的な劣化に対する単調な反応である [arxiv-1706.08500#c4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1706.08500v6#page=6 "The FID captures the disturbance level very well.")。

- **ImageNet のクラスに引きずられる**: FID の特徴空間は ImageNet の分類に近く、生成画像のクラスの分布を実画像に合わせるだけで、質を上げずに FID を下げられる [arxiv-2203.06026#c1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2203.06026v3#page=1 "We show that the feature space that FID is (typically) computed in is so close to the ImageNet classiﬁcations that aligning the histograms of Top-N classiﬁcations between sets of generated and real images can reduce FID substantially — without actually improving the quality of results.")。ImageNet に「人」や「顔」のクラスがない FFHQ では、FID が注目する領域は顔の外にあることが多い [arxiv-2203.06026#c3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2203.06026v3#page=4 "Interestingly, we observe that the heatmaps seem to correspond very well with the areas that the image’s ImageNet Top-1 class prediction would occupy.")。
- **サンプル数に依存する**: FID は実画像と生成画像の数に強く依存するので、数を揃える必要がある(先行研究を引いた記述) [arxiv-2203.06026#c9](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2203.06026v3#page=14 "FID depends strongly on the number of samples used in evaluation (Figure 8a).")。実際、一部の FID を少ないサンプル数で計算し、上方に偏ると認めている論文がある [arxiv-2102.09672#c7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.09672v1#page=11 "Since we mainly use FID for relative comparisons on unconditional ImageNet 64×64, this bias is acceptable.") [arxiv-2112.10752#c5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2112.10752v2#page=27 "For efﬁciency reasons we compute the sample quality metrics plotted in Fig. 6, 17 and 7 based on 5k samples.")。
- **人の評価と合わない**: Stein らは、大規模な人の評価実験で、既存の指標はどれも人の判断と強くは相関しないと報告した [arxiv-2306.04675#c1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.04675v2#page=1 "Using best practices in psychophysics, we measure human perception of image realism for generated samples by conducting the largest experiment evaluating generative models to date, and find that no existing metric strongly correlates with human evaluations.")。人は拡散モデルの画像を最も本物らしいと判断したが、Inception を使う FID は GAN を好んだ。自己教師ありの大規模なエンコーダで計算すると、そのずれは消えた [arxiv-2306.04675#c4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.04675v2#page=5 "Coupling the results in Table 1 with the FID rankings in Figure 2, we conclude that current diffusion models produce the most realistic images according to human perception, but are downranked by FID.") [arxiv-2306.04675#c5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.04675v2#page=7 "We find that diffusion models are often driving the discrepancy in alignment with human evaluation for the Inception network: FID prefers GANs over diffusion while FD determined by self-supervised models trained on very large and diverse datasets does not.")。
- **それでも使える場面**: FID が大きいことは学習の失敗を確実に示し、収束の監視にも使えそうだ、とされる [arxiv-2203.06026#c7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2203.06026v3#page=9 "The caveats arise when two sufﬁciently different architectures and/or training setups are compared.")。ImageNet で事前学習した部品を使うモデルでは、ImageNet で学習していない特徴空間でも確かめることが勧められている [arxiv-2203.06026#c8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2203.06026v3#page=9 "As a partial solution, the FID improvements should at least be veriﬁed using a non-ImageNet trained Fr´echet distance.")。

### 学習データの記憶と複製

- **抽出攻撃**: Carlini らは、学習画像とほぼ同じ画像を生成させられるかを、意図的に保守的な定義で調べた [arxiv-2301.13188#c1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2301.13188v1#page=4 "Instead, we deﬁne a notion of approximate memorization based on image similarity metrics.") [arxiv-2301.13188#c2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2301.13188v1#page=4 "Our deﬁnition of extraction is intentionally conservative as compared to what privacy concerns one might ultimately have.")。Stable Diffusion では、重複の多い学習画像に的を絞って探した [arxiv-2301.13188#c3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2301.13188v1#page=4 "To reduce the computational load of our attack, as is done in [65], we bias our search towards duplicated training examples because these are orders of magnitude more likely to be memorized than non-duplicated examples [47, 41].")。重複が記憶の大きな要因である [arxiv-2301.13188#c4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2301.13188v1#page=6 "These results show that duplication is a major factor behind training data extraction.")。CIFAR-10 では、同程度の FID の GAN より拡散モデルの方がよく記憶していた。FID が良くなるほど、どちらの系統も記憶が増えた [arxiv-2301.13188#c7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2301.13188v1#page=13 "Overall, we ﬁnd that diffusion models memorize more data than GANs, even when the GANs reach similar performance, e.g., the best DDPM model memorizes 2× more than StyleGAN-ADA but reaches the same FID.")。
- **複製の調査**: Somepalli らは、学習データが小さいと DDPM がしばしば複製し、データが増えると減ることを示した [arxiv-2212.03860#c4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2212.03860v3#page=5 "We observe that the diffusion models trained on smaller datasets tend to generate images that are copied from the training data.")。ImageNet の潜在拡散モデルでは目立った複製はなかった [arxiv-2212.03860#c5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2212.03860v3#page=6 "Qualitatively, we observe no signiﬁcant copying in any of the generations by this model.")。Stable Diffusion では一定の複製が見つかった [arxiv-2212.03860#c6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2212.03860v3#page=7 "Above this 0.5 threshold, we observe a signiﬁcant amount of copying.")。重複だけでは説明できない、とも述べている [arxiv-2212.03860#c8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2212.03860v3#page=10 "However this explanation is incomplete and oversimpliﬁed; Our models in Section 5 consistently show strong replication when they are trained with small datasets that are unlikely to have any duplicated images.")。
- **測り方の限界**: どこからを複製とみなすかは主観的である [arxiv-2212.03860#c2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2212.03860v3#page=2 "Rather than choosing an arbitrary deﬁnition, we focus on presenting quantitative and qualitative results to the reader, leaving each person to draw their own conclusions based on their role and stake in the process of generative AI.")。調べたのは学習データのごく一部なので、複製は過小評価されている [arxiv-2212.03860#c9](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2212.03860v3#page=11 "For both of these reasons, the results here systematically underestimate the amount of replication in Stable Diffusion and other models.")。既存の記憶の指標は信頼できない [arxiv-2306.04675#c8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.04675v2#page=10 "In conclusion, we find that none of AuthPct, the CT score, or FLS-POG is a reliable metric for memorization.")。抽出攻撃が成功しても、大規模モデルが新しいものを生成しているのか学習例を写して補間しているのかは決着しない [arxiv-2301.13188#c9](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2301.13188v1#page=15 "If our extraction attacks had failed, it may have refuted the hypothesis that models copy and interpolate training data; but because our attacks succeed, this question remains open.")。
- **対策**: 重複の除去は記憶を減らしたが、なくしはしなかった [arxiv-2301.13188#c8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2301.13188v1#page=14 "While not a substantial drop, these results show that deduplication can mitigate memorization.")。

## 設計への示唆(本記事の整理)

論文の主張そのものではなく、本記事のまとめである。

1. **尤度とサンプルの質のどちらを目的にするか決める**: 両者は一致せず、損失の重みやノイズの入れ方で入れ替わる [arxiv-2006.11239#c5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2006.11239v2#page=6 "We ﬁnd that training our models on the true variational bound yields better codelengths than training on the simpliﬁed objective, as expected, but the latter yields the best sample quality.") [arxiv-2102.09672#c5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.09672v1#page=5 "Optimizing Lvlb further improves log-likelihood at the cost of a higher FID.") [arxiv-2011.13456#c6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2011.13456v2#page=8 "As shown in Table 3, VE SDEs typically provide better sample quality than VP/sub-VP SDEs, but we also empirically observe that their likelihoods are worse than VP/sub-VP SDE counterparts.")。
2. **まず学習し直さないサンプラーを試す**: DDIM や DPM-Solver は学習済みモデルにそのまま使える [arxiv-2010.02502#c2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2010.02502v4#page=4 "Therefore, we can essentially use pretrained DDPM models as the solutions to the new objectives, and focus on ﬁnding a generative process that is better at producing samples subject to our needs by changing σ.") [arxiv-2206.00927#c2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2206.00927v3#page=1 "DPM-Solver is suitable for both discrete-time and continuous-time DPMs without any further training.")。少ない段数がさらに必要なら蒸留を検討する [arxiv-2202.00512#c6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2202.00512v2#page=7 "At 2 or 1 sampling steps, the sample quality degrades relatively more quickly.")。
3. **ガイダンスの強さは用途で決める**: 多様性と忠実さの引き換えで、最良点は指標によって違う [arxiv-2207.12598#c6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2207.12598v1#page=7 "We obtain the best FID results with a small amount of guidance (w = 0.1 or w = 0.3, depending on the dataset) and the best IS result with strong guidance (w ≥4).")。
4. **FID だけで比べない**: サンプル数を揃え、ImageNet 以外の特徴空間や人の評価でも確かめる [arxiv-2203.06026#c9](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2203.06026v3#page=14 "FID depends strongly on the number of samples used in evaluation (Figure 8a).") [arxiv-2203.06026#c8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2203.06026v3#page=9 "As a partial solution, the FID improvements should at least be veriﬁed using a non-ImageNet trained Fr´echet distance.") [arxiv-2306.04675#c1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.04675v2#page=1 "Using best practices in psychophysics, we measure human perception of image realism for generated samples by conducting the largest experiment evaluating generative models to date, and find that no existing metric strongly correlates with human evaluations.")。
5. **学習データの重複を除き、記憶を確かめる**: 重複は記憶の大きな要因である [arxiv-2301.13188#c4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2301.13188v1#page=6 "These results show that duplication is a major factor behind training data extraction.") [arxiv-2301.13188#c8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2301.13188v1#page=14 "While not a substantial drop, these results show that deduplication can mitigate memorization.")。

## わかっていないこと

- **尤度とサンプルの質がなぜ一致しないか**: 損失の重みに関する仮説はある [arxiv-2107.00630#c7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2107.00630v6#page=21 "The latter two works report better FID and Inception Score than Song et al. [2020] and the current paper, which we hypothesize is due to their loss emphasizing the global consistence and coarse level patterns more than the ﬁne scale features of the data.") が、この範囲では検証されていない。
- **記憶と汎化の境界**: 大規模モデルが新しいものを生成しているのか、写して補間しているのかは未解決である [arxiv-2301.13188#c9](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2301.13188v1#page=15 "If our extraction attacks had failed, it may have refuted the hypothesis that models copy and interpolate training data; but because our attacks succeed, this question remains open.")。
- **人の判断に合う評価指標**: 既存の指標はどれも人の判断と強くは相関しない [arxiv-2306.04675#c1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.04675v2#page=1 "Using best practices in psychophysics, we measure human perception of image realism for generated samples by conducting the largest experiment evaluating generative models to date, and find that no existing metric strongly correlates with human evaluations.")。人の評価も「本物らしさ」だけを測っていて、多様性や記憶は対象外である [arxiv-2306.04675#c2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.04675v2#page=4 "Each trial is a two alternative forced choice task where a participant is shown either a generated image from one model, or an image from the training dataset, and must choose if it is real or fake.")。
- **順方向の過程の終わらせ方**: 有限時間で情報を完全に消すことはできず、いつ止めるかは未解決の方向として挙げられている [arxiv-2209.00796#c7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2209.00796v15#page=44 "In reality, complete removal of information is unachievable in finite time.")。

## 現時点での整理

- **DDPM、スコアベース、SDE は、ノイズを加える過程とスコアの推定という共通の枠組みで統一された** [arxiv-2006.11239#c1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2006.11239v2#page=2 "We obtained our best sample quality results using this parameterization (Section 4.2), so we consider this equivalence to be one of our primary contributions.")
