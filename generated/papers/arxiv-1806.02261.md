## 主張

出典のリンクは原論文PDFの該当ページを開く。リンクにカーソルを合わせると原文の引用が表示される。

- **c1** Motivation: BOCPD methods declare changepoints when the posterior predictive has low density for the next observation, which the authors say naturally leads to a high false changepoint discovery rate under outliers; because they run on-line, pre-processing (outlier removal) is not an option. Standard Bayes (KLD) is not robust under outliers or misspecification because its influence function is strictly increasing.([Introduction, p.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1806.02261v2#page=1 "Naturally, this leads to a high false CP discovery rate in the presence of outliers and as they run on-line, pre-processing is not an option."))
- **c2** Robust BOCPD: both the run-length/model posterior and the parameter posteriors are replaced by β-divergence posteriors with β = (β_rlm, β_p) > 0. β_rlm prevents abrupt changes of the run-length posterior caused by a small number of observations; for the run-length part one simply replaces the predictive densities in the BOCPD recursion by their β-D counterparts, and the discrete posterior remains easy to normalize. β_p regulates the robustness of the parameter posterior, which is intractable and is handled by structural variational inference.([Robust BOCPD, p.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1806.02261v2#page=4 "This form of robustness is easy to implement and retains the closed forms of BOCPD:"))
- **c3** Why heavy tails are not enough: the run-length odds after an outlier compare the predictive density under the current segment with the density under the prior; a Student's t error model raises both, so changing the tails has only a very limited effect on the ratio. The authors state the perhaps unintuitive consequence that Student's t error models yield changepoint inference very close to the normal model (numerical examples in the appendix).([Section 2.3 (Quantifying robustness), p.5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1806.02261v2#page=5 "In fact, the perhaps unintuitive consequence is that Student’s t error models will yield CP inference that very closely resembles that of the corresponding normal model."))
- **c4** Theorem 1 (for the Bayesian linear regression model): β_rlm and the hazard can be chosen from a set (defined by an inequality in the appendix) so that the odds never favour a changepoint after any single outlying observation. It requires a lower bound |V|_min > 0 on the determinant of the posterior predictive variance, because the integral term of the β-D loss dominates if |V| is extremely small; the authors say this is not restrictive in practice.([Section 2.3 (Quantifying robustness), p.5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1806.02261v2#page=5 "Theorem 1 provides very mild conditions for the β-D robustiﬁed BLR model ensuring that the odds never favor a CP after any single outlying observation"))
- **c5** Computation: sampling-based General Bayesian inference would make the algorithm on-line in name only and add three sources of approximation error per (run length, model) and time step, so the β-D posterior is approximated within the conjugate family by structural variational inference (exact as β → 0). Full optimization of the variational parameters at every step costs O(T) space and O(T^2) time; the proposed SVRG variant (full optimization every m steps for the first W iterations of a run length, then anchored stochastic updates on a window) has constant space and linear time.([Stochastic Variance Reduced Gradient (SVRG) for BOCPD, p.7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1806.02261v2#page=7 "The resulting on-line inference has constant space and linear time complexity like SGD, but produces good estimates for small rt and converges faster"))
- **c6** Choosing β: the authors note there was no principled framework for selecting β. β_p is initialized by solving for the β_p whose point of maximum influence lies at a chosen Mahalanobis distance under the prior (k-th standard deviation for low dimensions, sqrt(d) for larger d); β_rlm initialization matters less (values in [0, 1] give reasonable results). β is then refined on-line by SGD on a predictive loss (a bounded absolute loss in the experiments), with the gradient for β_p approximated numerically. Because the losses need not be convex in β_p, initial values can matter.([Section 4 (Choice of β), p.7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1806.02261v2#page=7 "We remedy this by minimizing the expected predictive loss with respect to β on-line."))
- **c7** Well-log (univariate benchmark whose outliers are usually removed before running CP detection): the maximum of the standard BOCPD run-length distribution is zero 145 times, so declaring CPs at the run-length maximum gives a false discovery rate > 90%; with MAP segmentation standard BOCPD mislabels 8 outliers as CPs (FDR > 40%), whereas the β-D version mislabels none. The robust version has more computational overhead but needs less than 0.5 seconds per observation (3.1 GHz Intel i7, 16GB RAM).([Section 5 (Well-log), p.8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1806.02261v2#page=8 "Even using Maximum A Posteriori (MAP) segmentation [11], standard BOCPD mislabels 8 outliers"))
- **c8** Protocol caveat for the well-log comparison: standard and robust BOCPD were run with different priors. Because of Theorem 1 the robust version can use a much less informative prior; for the standard version a more informative prior (constant hazard 0.01) was used, because with too flat priors it declares far too many changepoints.([Appendix I.1 (Well-log data), p.34](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1806.02261v2#page=34 "If priors are too ﬂat, the standard version declares far too many changepoints."))
- **c9** Air pollution (NOX at 29 London stations, spatially structured Bayesian VARs; earlier robust on-line methods cannot be applied because they assume univariate or independent data): robust BOCPD finds one CP corresponding to the congestion charge introduction while standard BOCPD has FDR > 90%. Both find a change in dynamics (models) after the congestion charge, with substantially lower model-posterior variance for the robust algorithm.([Section 5.2 (Air Pollution), p.9](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1806.02261v2#page=9 "As Figure 5 shows, robust BOCPD ﬁnds one CP corresponding to the introduction of the congestion charge, while standard BOCPD produces an FDR >90%."))
