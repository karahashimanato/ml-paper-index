## 主張

- **c1** Failure mode: the authors identify 12 NAS benchmarks based on four search spaces in which standard DARTS yields degenerate architectures with poor test performance across several datasets.([Introduction, p.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1909.09656v2#page=1 "We identify 12 NAS benchmarks based on four search spaces in which standard DARTS yields degenerate architectures with poor test performance across several datasets (Section 3)."))
- **c2** Running DARTS on S1-S4 with exactly the same setup as Liu et al. (2019), in the found CIFAR-10 cells parameter-less skip connections dominate almost all edges for S1-S3, and in S4 the deliberately harmful Noise operation was selected for five of eight operations.([Section 3, p.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1909.09656v2#page=4 "the parameter-less skip connections dominate in almost all the edges for spaces S1-S3, and for S4 even the harmful Noise operation was selected for ﬁve out of eight operations."))
- **c3** The authors stress that S1-S3 are natural strict subspaces of the original DARTS space that should be easier to search and were not constructed adversarially; only S4 was constructed specifically to show-case the failure mode.([Section 3, p.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1909.09656v2#page=4 "We emphasize that search spaces S1-S3 are very natural, and, as strict subspaces of the original space, should merely be easier to search than that."))
- **c4** On S5 (81 architectures, all evaluated a priori), DARTS finds an architecture close to the global minimum but test performance deteriorates around epoch 40 while the search model validation error keeps converging, indicating overfitting of the architectural parameters to the validation set; random search with weight sharing (RS-ws) stays relatively constant and, evaluating only the final architecture, outperformed DARTS.([Section 3, p.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1909.09656v2#page=4 "indicating that the archi-tectural parameters are overﬁtting to the vali-dation set."))
- **c5** The dominant eigenvalue of the Hessian of the validation loss w.r.t. the architectural parameters (full Hessian on a randomly sampled mini-batch, used as a sharpness proxy) increases during standard DARTS along with test error; across 24 architectures on S1/CIFAR-10 it strongly correlates with test error (Pearson coefficient 0.867).([Section 4.1, p.5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1909.09656v2#page=5 "indeed strongly correlates with test error (with a Pearson correlation coefﬁcient of 0.867)."))
- **c6** Early stopping (DARTS-ES): since NAS directly optimizes validation performance, stopping on validation performance does not apply; instead the smoothed dominant eigenvalue (over k = 5 epochs) is tracked and the search stops when it increases too much (ratio below 0.75), returning the architecture from k epochs earlier. The authors describe this as a simple heuristic that worked off-the-shelf without any tuning, and state the test data is never used for early stopping.([Section 4.3, p.6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1909.09656v2#page=6 "To implement this idea, we use a simple heuristic that worked off-the-shelf without any tuning."))
- **c7** Regularization of the inner objective: hypothesizing that changing the landscape of the training loss could redirect the architectural parameters to flatter regions, the authors study data augmentation (Cutout and ScheduledDropPath) and stronger L2 regularization during the search phase only; the final training and evaluation phase regularization is not altered.([Section 5, p.7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1909.09656v2#page=7 "We emphasize that we do not alter the regularization of the ﬁnal training and evaluation phase, but solely that of the search phase."))
- **c8** Comparison with random search: each method was searched four times with different seeds and selected by a validation run (as proposed in the DARTS paper); in accordance with Li & Talwalkar (2019), RS-ws often outperformed the original DARTS, but with the proposed robustifications DARTS typically performed substantially better than RS-ws. The authors recommend RobustDARTS for practice as it performed best and is easier to implement than DARTS-ES and DARTS-ADA.([Section 5.3, p.9](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1909.09656v2#page=9 "As the table shows, in accordance with Li & Talwalkar (2019), RS-ws often outperformed the original DARTS; however, with our robustiﬁcations, DARTS typically performs substantially better than RS-ws."))
- **c9** On the full original DARTS search spaces, RobustDARTS performed similarly to DARTS on the two original benchmarks (PTB and CIFAR-10), on which DARTS was developed and is well tuned, but significantly better when only the dataset was changed to CIFAR-100 or SVHN, which the authors take as underlining its robustness.([Section 5.3, p.9](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1909.09656v2#page=9 "RobustDARTS performed similarly to DARTS for the two original benchmarks from the DARTS paper (PTB and CIFAR-10), on which DARTS was developed and is well tuned;"))
